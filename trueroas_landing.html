Frequently asked questions

Is TrueROAS really local‑first? Where does my data go?
Yes. All reconciliation happens in a sandbox on your own computer. We never receive your raw order data, customer PII, or ad account details. Only optional, aggregated metrics may be shared if you choose benchmarking features in the future.
How do you connect to Shopify and Meta safely?
You connect through official Shopify and Meta OAuth flows. We request read‑only API scopes only. That means we cannot create ads, change budgets, or modify store settings. You stay in full control.
How long does setup take?
Most brands are fully set up in under 5 minutes. Download the app, run the installer, connect your accounts, and start your first reconciliation. Early access licenses include a live onboarding call if you want help.
Can I uninstall and remove all data?
Yes. All stored data lives on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes your local data. We do not keep backups or copies on our side.
Is this a subscription or a one‑time purchase?
It is a one‑time lifetime license. There are no recurring fees. Your license includes 12 months of updates; after that, you can keep using your current version indefinitely.
